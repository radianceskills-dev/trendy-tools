From text to flowchart in three steps

  1. Describe the process. Use specific steps and name the decision conditions. For a yes/no branch, say where each outcome leads.
  2. Generate a draft. Connect a provider in AI setup, return to the editor, and choose Create diagram. The AI produces D2 source for the diagram.
  3. Review and refine. Check for missing steps, edit labels and connections, and verify that every path reaches the intended outcome.

Why use text-based flowcharts?

D2 describes diagrams with readable text. Instead of dragging every connector, you can change a relationship such as request -> review and let the renderer handle the layout. This is useful for repeatable processes, technical documentation, and quickly revising a draft.

AI helps turn an unstructured description into that first draft. It does not validate your business rules: review branching conditions, exception paths, and responsibilities before sharing the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI flowchart generator free?

Trendy Tools does not charge for access to its diagram editor. AI requests use the provider configured in your browser. Free usage may be available through that provider, but quotas, account requirements, and charges are controlled by the provider—not by this page.

Can I edit the generated diagram?

Yes. Generated diagrams use D2 source that you can revise in the editor. Change node labels, add connections, and correct the process after generation.

Can I use the flowchart tool offline?

AI generation requires an internet connection. Local editing and rendering are separate from AI, and offline availability depends on whether the required editor resources have already loaded. Test your intended workflow before disconnecting.

Where does my prompt go?

When you generate with AI, your prompt is sent to your selected provider. Do not include confidential information unless you are comfortable with that provider’s data handling. Opening this page or selecting an example does not automatically send an AI request.

Which flowcharts can I create?

Start with approvals, onboarding, customer support, troubleshooting, or other processes with steps and decisions. Complex diagrams may need manual corrections to the D2 source.
